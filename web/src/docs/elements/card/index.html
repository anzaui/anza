Card

A bordered panel with header, body and footer areas.

Tag: ui-card · Import: @anzaui/anza/elements/card

Usage

import '@anzaui/anza/elements/card'; <ui-card> <h3 slot="header">Plan</h3> <p>Pro, billed monthly.</p> <ui-button slot="footer">Change</ui-button> </ui-card>

Attributes

AttributeTypeDefaultDescription
interactiveBooleanfalsePointer cursor, and on hover the card lifts, its shadow grows and its border darkens.

Slots

SlotContent
headerTop area, divided from the body by a border
defaultThe body
footerBottom area, right-aligned, on the page surface colour

The header and footer areas keep their padding and border even when their slot is empty.

Parts

PartElement
